---
name: My Space calendar content deletion
description: Permission and scope rules for deleting session content from My Space calendar.
---

Staff Delete permission is independently configurable in Settings so assigned staff can remove content they need to replace. View All remains hidden and unavailable. Deletion is only for individual session content; it must not permit student removal or deletion of every item in a session. Education behavior stays unchanged.

**Why:** The user clarified that Delete is needed to replace assigned content, but broad deletion and access to other staff's sessions remain out of scope.

**How to apply:** Each My Space delete request must verify the Delete permission, effective staff assignment to the session, and that the content belongs to that session. For student-specific free-class content, also verify the staff member is effectively assigned to that student for the session.
